Saturday Sounds: Chris Thile

20 September 2025, 6:30pm
Music Room, Burgh House

Join us for a special concert with mandolinist Chris Thile.

Christopher Scott Thile is an American mandolinist, singer, songwriter, composer, and radio personality, best known for his work in the progressive acoustic trio Nickel Creek and the acoustic folk and progressive bluegrass quintet Punch Brothers. He is a 2012 MacArthur Fellow. He will perform a selection of classical and contemporary works in the beautiful setting of Burgh House's Music Room.

Tickets: £45 standard / £38 Friends of Burgh House / £35 under 18s
